Fits content_aspect within an avail_width x avail_height box, centered ("contain" fit – shown at the largest uniform scale that still fits without cropping, with the leftover margin on whichever axis doesn't exactly fit split evenly on both sides), so content is never distorted to fill a differently-shaped box.

x/y/width/height are relative to the avail box's own top-left corner; the caller adds whatever that box's own actual origin is.
