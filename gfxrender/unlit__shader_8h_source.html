11// Given baseline shading style: flat, unlit color -- no lighting
12// calculation at all. The simplest possible shading style, and a useful
13// debugging aid (e.g. to check raw vertex colors/texturing without any
14// lighting interaction obscuring them). Given from Shapes (3) on, ahead
15// of Camera & Shading (4)'s own PhongShader/HighlightShader (see
16// gfx_soln_libs/gfxshaders/) and its sibling GouraudShader here.
17//
18// Draws a Mesh and reads its vertex attributes at the same fixed
19// locations every other shader here uses (position=0, normal=1, color=2,
20// texcoord=3), since Mesh's GPU-side vertex layout (see mesh.cc's
21// UpdateGPUMemory()) is baked in at those locations regardless of which
22// shader ends up drawing it.
23
