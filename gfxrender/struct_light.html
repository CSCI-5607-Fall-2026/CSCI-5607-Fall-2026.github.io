Detailed Description

A single light, either a point light (finite position, shines equally in every direction, distance-limited shadows) or a directional light (a fixed direction with no position, as if from an infinitely distant source like the sun – shadows aren't distance-limited, since there's no finite distance to the source). Only position (point) or direction (directional) is meaningful, depending on type; the other is unused.
