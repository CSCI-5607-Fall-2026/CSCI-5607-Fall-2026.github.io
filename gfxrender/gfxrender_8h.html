Detailed Description

Includes the entire GfxRender library.

GfxRender holds the GfxCore pieces that need GfxMath (Mesh, the shader classes) – given to every assignment from Shapes onward, but not needed by Brush/Filter.

UnlitShader/GouraudShader are the given baseline shading styles, usable from Shapes (3) on; PhongShader/HighlightShader (the Camera & Shading (4) assignment's own content) join them from iModel (5) on, via GfxShaders. All four share MaterialProperties (UnlitShader also needs it, for its texture/diffuse color), defined here since GfxShaders depends on GfxRender and not the other way around. GouraudShader/PhongShader/HighlightShader (every shader that actually lights something) each overload Draw() to take either a PointLight or a DirectionalLight, also defined here for the same reason.

Material is a loader/holder built on top of MaterialProperties – LoadFromFile() reads a Wavefront .mtl file (the format used throughout gfx_libs/gfxrender/data), matching the Ns/Ka/Kd/Ks/map_Kd names shown in a course-authored material panel to the shaders' own uniform names.

SceneIO (scene_io.h) reads/writes a small JSON scene-description format – a flat list of objects, each with its own mesh/transform/ material – given from Camera & Shading (4) on. Plain data in, plain data out: a consuming app loops over the result itself and wires it to its own scene representation, rather than SceneIO building Mesh objects itself.

DebugGfx (with its BuildDebugLineMesh() helper) draws portable stand-ins for GL_LINES/GL_POINTS – both native primitives have real, unresolvable portability problems in this codebase's OpenGL 3.3 core profile target (glLineWidth > 1 is unsupported on macOS and unreliable elsewhere; GL_POINTS has the same story for point size), so both are built and drawn as ordinary triangle geometry instead.

camera_panels.h/scene_panel.h/scene_edit_panels.h are the given, cross-assignment GUI panel classes (camera presets/fly-orbit/direct- editing parameters; scene load/save; material/lights/background- color/object-creation/object-manipulation) shared by every assignment from Camera & Shading (4) through Ray (6) that needs them. Each panel is a plain, self-contained ImGui value holder (no dependency on Camera or this library's own scene types beyond tool_button.h's shared button/dropdown/floating-window infrastructure, itself in GfxCore since it has no GfxMath/GfxRender dependency of its own) – every consuming app's own code is the one place that converts to/from its own scene representation and Camera/SceneMaterialDesc/ Light.
