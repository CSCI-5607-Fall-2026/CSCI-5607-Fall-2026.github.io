11// Given baseline shading style: classic per-vertex lighting -- the Phong
12// reflection model is evaluated once per vertex, in the vertex shader,
13// and the result is simply interpolated (not recomputed) across each
14// triangle's fragments. Coarser highlights than PhongShader (see
15// gfx_soln_libs/gfxshaders/), especially on low-poly meshes -- the classic
16// Gouraud-vs-Phong comparison. Given from Shapes (3) on, ahead of Camera
17// & Shading (4)'s own PhongShader/HighlightShader.
18//
19// Draws a Mesh and reads its vertex attributes at the same fixed
20// locations every other shader here uses (position=0, normal=1, color=2,
21// texcoord=3), since Mesh's GPU-side vertex layout (see mesh.cc's
22// UpdateGPUMemory()) is baked in at those locations regardless of which
23// shader ends up drawing it.
